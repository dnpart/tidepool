Pond Hop · mockups, round three

Rocky edges, piers, and a koi pond

No shore and no coves. The stones the track runs on are the pond's rocky edge, the home lanes still run out on piers, and your lily pads float in the gaps at the four hop spots. Plus a new koi pond, and a Koi palette to go with it.

Each pond

One rock per hole, each a little different, so the ring reads as a stone edge rather than a row of dots: sandy in Lagoon, snow-capped in Winter, mossy in the Forest, and wax comb cells around the honey. The new koi pond has smooth river stones, three koi, and piers like a garden bridge: pale boards between red rails.

Mid-hop

The blue marble hopping between lily pads: ripples on water, a skate mark on ice, a honey trail in honey. Open water all the way across.

A Koi palette, made for the cats

I think it's a lovely pairing: cats and a koi pond tell a little story on their own. One thing to know: palettes and decks are separate settings, so Koi would work with every deck, but it can be designed with the cats in mind, above all in its Cats card back. The look is a Japanese garden: stone and moss greens, a red-orange accent like a garden bridge, cherry petals drifting down by day, and paper lanterns glowing at night.
