Pond Hop · mockups, round two

Shore and piers

The pond still fills nearly the whole circle, but the track now runs around its shore, and the home lanes run out over the water on piers. Your marbles and lily pads are in every board.

What this fixes

  • Hops cross open water. Nothing stands in the middle any more, so the ripples, the skate mark and the honey trail all happen over the pond. The mid-hop boards below show it.
  • Even spacing. The track holes sit on the shore the way they do today, evenly round the ring, with nothing squeezed under them. Each home lane is a straight pier with its four spots evenly along it.
  • Nothing adrift. The track is on land, the lanes are on piers, and your marbles finish at the end of a pier, out over the water.
  • Lily pads in coves. The pond reaches out into the shore at the four hop spots, so your lily pads float in water right on the track.

Each palette

Lagoon has a sandy shore and wooden piers. Winter has snow, ice and snowy boardwalks. Forest has a grassy shore with reeds, and fallen logs for piers. In Honeycomb the shore is wax comb, with one cell for each hole, and the piers are strips of comb.
