Pond Hop · mockups

Ponds that fill the circle

The pond grows from a small pool in the middle to the whole circle, so the track runs around on the water. Your marbles and lily pads are in every board, mid-game, so you can judge how marbles read on each pond.

Is there a reason not to?

Not a strong one; it was just how the first mockup was drawn. The real catch is the one you spotted: once everything is water, the holes and home lanes float with nothing under them, and a blue marble on blue water gets hard to see. So every spot needs something to sit on. These two looks solve that in different ways:

  • The middle becomes an island. The home lanes run in to it, so the last home spot sits on its shore and your marbles finish on land, not adrift.
  • The corners stay on shore. They sit outside the circle, on the board's edge, as now.
  • Your four lily pads stay the hop spots, so the hop still reads as a jump across the water.
A

Stepping stones in every pond

Every hole sits on a flat stone, the same idea in every palette: snow-capped on the ice, mossy in the forest, little wax drops in the honey. Calm and consistent.

B

Each pond its own way

Lagoon keeps the stepping stones. Winter's holes are cut into the ice, like ice fishing. The forest track runs over small lily pads. The honey track is wax comb cells. More character, a little busier, shown in dark mode too.
