Forest and Honeycomb ponds

Two looks for each palette's pond, in light and dark, plus what a hop could do on each. As with the ice, only the pond changes; the board, marbles, holes and targets stay as they are, and the lily pads on the track stay lily pads.

Forest

A

Fallen leaves

Deep green water with a few autumn leaves floating on it and reeds with cattails at the edge. In dark mode, three fireflies glow over the water.

Hop: the marble skips across like a stone, leaving three ripple rings. Sound: three quick plips.

B

Duckweed

A still pond carpeted with tiny duckweed, open water showing through here and there. In dark mode, the fireflies again.

Hop: the marble parts the duckweed, leaving a clear lane that drifts closed. Sound: today's splash.

Honeycomb

A

Honey pool

The pond is a pool of honey with a glossy shine and a few bubbles, set in a ring of comb cells. The middle lily pad becomes three little comb cells.

Hop: the marble leaves a sticky honey trail that slowly sinks back in. Sound: a thick gloop.

B

Golden hour

Water in warm late-afternoon light, with pollen and two petals floating on it.

Hop: soft golden ripples where it skips, as in Forest A. Sound: today's splash.
