Frozen pond

Two looks for the pond when Winter is on, each in light and dark, next to today's pond. Only the pond and the lily pads change. The board, marbles, holes and glowing targets stay as they are.

Today

The pond is the same pale water in every palette. In Winter dark it's the brightest thing on the board.

Clear ice

Pale ice with a frosty edge, a few cracks and glints, a little snow banked on the rim. The middle lily pad is frozen in, with a snow clump where the flower was, and the four lily pads on the track get a dusting of snow.

Snowy ice

The same ice with drifts of snow lying on it and more snow piled round the edge. Cozier, a little busier.

A hop on ice

A hop already slides the marble straight across the pond. On ice it could leave a faint skate mark that fades, and play a short swish instead of the splash.
